p1:l31 This course provides an overview of some research fields of computer science to bridge fundamental theories of
p1:l32 computer science with the cutting-edge research in our department. The course consists of algorithm theory,
p1:l33 communication network, and parallel computing.
p1:l34 Students will come to understand the association between the fundamental knowledge about computer science and the
p1:l35 research activities. The emphasis will be on giving a broad overview of the research areas in computer science.
p1:l36 Please see Google Classroom. Class code: dhjxhjdp
p1:l37 01. Introduction of this course
p1:l38 (The order of the parts is subject to change.)
p1:l39 [Algorithm Theory]
p1:l40 Algorithms now play a very important role for the reliability and efficiency in several social systems. In this part, we focus
p1:l41 on design and analysis of algorithms from the viewpoint of theoretical computer science. The main topics are as follows:
p1:l42 02. What is algorithm? Power of "efficient" algorithms
p1:l43 03. How to evaluate algorithms
p1:l44 04. Basic techniques for algorithms (1)
p1:l45 05. Basic techniques for algorithms (2)
p1:l46 06. Algorithms for computationally hard problems
p1:l47 07. Online algorithms
p1:l48 [Communication Network]
p1:l49 Communication network infrastructure needs to be resilient to natural disasters. In this part, we focus on the information
p1:l50 and communication technology for disaster response and recovery. The main topics are as follows:
p1:l51 08. Basic technologies for communication networks
p1:l52 09. UAV network
p1:l53 10. Wireless mesh network
p1:l54 11. Device to Device (D2D) network
p1:l55 [Parallel Computing]
p1:l56 Parallel computing is extremely important in many fields such as numerical simulations, artificial intelligence, machine
p1:l57 learning, optimizations, etc. In this part, we focus on leaning different parallel computation techniques, their bottlenecks
p1:l58 and how to simply run a program in parallel. The main topics are as follows:
p1:l59 12. Basics of parallel computing
p1:l60 13. CPU-based multicore computing
p1:l61 14. GPU parallel computing
p1:l62 15. Introduction to FPGA-based parallel computing
p1:l63 Students are required to make a thorough review. They are also expected to learn topics related to the class by
p1:l64 themselves. Such related topics and keywords will be provided during the lectures.
