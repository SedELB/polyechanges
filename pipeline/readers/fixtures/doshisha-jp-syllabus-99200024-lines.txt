p1:l14 ＜概要/Course Content Summary＞
p1:l15 This course introduces students to essential statistical concepts and methods used in the social
p1:l16 sciences and humanities. Emphasis is placed on understanding statistical reasoning, selecting
p1:l17 appropriate analytical tools, and interpreting quantitative findings in social, cultural, political,
p1:l18 and humanistic research contexts. Rather than focusing on advanced mathematics, the course
p1:l19 prioritizes practical application, critical thinking, and responsible use of data.
p1:l20 Students will work with real-world datasets, learn to evaluate statistical claims in academic
p1:l21 literature and public discourse, and develop a small-scale research project that integrates theory,
p1:l22 data, and statistical analysis. By the end of the course, students will be equipped to both
p1:l23 conduct and critically assess quantitative research relevant to their fields.
p1:l24 ＜到達⽬標/Goals,Aims＞
p1:l25 Course Objectives
p1:l26 By the end of this course, students will be able to:
p1:l27 •Understand fundamental statistical concepts and terminology
p1:l28 •Apply descriptive and inferential statistical methods to social science and humanities data
p1:l29 •Interpret statistical results and communicate findings clearly in written and oral form
p1:l30 •Critically evaluate quantitative research in academic literature
p1:l31 •Design and conduct a basic empirical research project
p1:l32 •Use statistical software at an introductory level (e.g., SPSS, R, Excel, or equivalent)
