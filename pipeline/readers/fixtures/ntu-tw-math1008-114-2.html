Course Description

This course bridges the gap between the computation-focused calculus courses (MATH4006–4009) and rigorous mathematical foundations, responding to the growing interest among students from diverse fields—for instance, physics students who work intensively with Hilbert spaces and economics students who require point-set topology for advanced courses.

The course introduces essential concepts of analysis, including metric spaces (exemplified by R^n with the Euclidean metric and function spaces with the supremum metric), convergence, continuity, connectedness, and compactness. We will demonstrate that the last two properties are preserved under continuous mappings, thereby generalizing two fundamental theorems from elementary calculus—the Intermediate Value Theorem and the Extreme Value Theorem.

Students will gain experience in rigorous argumentation, preparing them for advanced mathematics courses.

本課程旨於銜接目前以計算為主的微積分課程（MATH4006–4009）與更嚴謹的數學課程，回應來自多元領域學生日益增長的需求——例如，需要了解希爾伯特空間（Hilbert Space) 的物理系學生，以及需要點集拓撲 (Point Set Topology) 以修讀更高階課程的經濟系學生。

課程將介紹分析的核心概念，包括賦距空間（以歐幾里得度量的 R^n 與以上確界度量的函數空間為例）、數列收斂性、函數連續性、集合連通性與緊緻性。我們將說明集合連通性和緊緻性在連續映射下依然保持不變，推廣基礎微積分中的兩個重要定理——中間值定理與極值定理。

透過這門課程，同學將累積嚴謹數學論證經驗，為日後更高階的數學課程做好準備。

Provisional Syllabus :
- Metric spaces (Definition of a metric, Examples)
- Convergence of sequences (Definition, Examples)
- Open and closed sets (Definition of open & closed balls, open & closed sets, limit points, interior & closure)
- Continuous functions (three definitions : classical, sequential continuity and in terms of preimage of open sets)
- Complete metric spaces (Cauchy sequences, Axiom of completeness of R, proof that C[a,b] is complete)
- Contraction Mapping Theorem (Statement and proof)
- Compactness (Definition, sequential compactness, compactness preserved by continuity, EVT)
- Weierstrass Approximation Theorem (Statement and examples)
- Connectedness (Connectedness and Path-connectedness, connectedness preserved by continuity, IVT)
- Arzelà-Ascoli Theorem (Equicontinuity, statement and proof) 

Course Objective
 Upon completion of this course, students should be able to

1) demonstrate understanding of the basic concepts, theorems and calculations of metric spaces;
2) demonstrate understanding of the various definitions of continuity and applications to open or closed sets;
3) demonstrate understanding of the basic concepts, theorems and calculations of the concepts of Compactness, Connectedness and Completeness.

Moreover, students are expected to be capable of pursuing further theoretical courses on analysis, differential equations and geometry. 
Course Requirement
 We expect students enrolled in the course to have :
1) satisfactory results in MATH4006-7.
2) ability to read set notations.
3) interests in learning and writing rigorous mathematical arguments. 
Student Workload (Expected weekly study hours before and/or after class)
 Besides the 2-hour lectures per week, students should expect to spend around 2-3 hours weekly in digesting the lecture materials as well as completing exercises offered by the lecturer or the teaching assistant(s).
