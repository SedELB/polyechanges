p1:l15 ＜概要/Course Content Summary＞
p1:l16 This course will teach presentation skills for academic and professional purposes. We will
p1:l17 examine different types of presentations and learn to use the appropriate format for any given
p1:l18 situation.
p1:l19 The importance of body language， voice delivery and use of gestures will be covered. A study
p1:l20 of the structure of a presentation will follow， emphasizing the importance of a strong
p1:l21 introduction， body and a succinct conclusion. Finally， the effective use of visuals such as
p1:l22 graphs and pictures will be examined.
p1:l23 The course will make extensive use of PowerPoint as a presentation aid (prior experience is not
p1:l24 necessary).
p1:l25 Students will be assessed via group and individual presentations will which take place at
p1:l26 various points in the semester.
p1:l27 IMPORTANT! This course is conducted in English. Students should be able to read course
p1:l28 materials and understand spoken English. Furthermore， students must be willing to give
p1:l29 presentations in English.
p1:l30 ＜到達⽬標/Goals,Aims＞
p1:l31 Students will learn to identify the common mistakes made in presentations and how to avoid
p1:l32 them. They will learn the concepts of structure and good slide design.
p1:l33 Additionally， students will gain confidence in the use of spoken English when giving
p1:l34 presentations to a group of their peers.
