p1:l35 Mathematics B
p1:l36 Themes are chosen from algebra, geometry, and analysis and are explained in English by experts in each field,
p1:l37 allowing students to learn mathematics and how to express it in English.
p1:l38 Also the student is assumed to be familiar with the subjects normally taught in classes to math majors by the first half
p1:l39 of the 3rd year.
p1:l40 This course will cover some advanced and basic mathematics. Students will gain perspective and an understanding of
p1:l41 the usefulness of mathematics.
p1:l42 ALGEBRA "Linear representations of finite groups" /Masaki Hanamura
p1:l43 1. Review of group theory; linear representations of groups; equivalence, irreducibility, unitary representations.
p1:l44 2. Complete reducibility; homomorphisms of representations.
p1:l45 3. Schur's theorem, Schur orthogonality.
p1:l46 4. Characters of representations, decompositions into irreducible representations and multiplicity.
p1:l47 5. Regular representations and the second orthogonality of characters.
p1:l48 Analysis “Spectral decomposition theorem for self adjoint operators” / Fumihiko Nakano
p1:l49 1. Background and motivation : basic principles of quantum mechanics
p1:l50 2. Preliminaries : basic facts on Hilbert spaces
p1:l51 3. Spectral decomposition on cyclic spaces
p1:l52 4. Spectral decomposition on general cases
p1:l53 5. Application : spectral projection ; RAGE theorem ; min-max principle
p1:l54 GEOMETRY "Introduction to the Hausdorff measure and dimension" / Takumi Yokota
p1:l55 1. Definitions
p1:l56 2. Examples
p1:l57 3. Properties
p1:l58 4. Applications
p1:l59 5. Related topics
