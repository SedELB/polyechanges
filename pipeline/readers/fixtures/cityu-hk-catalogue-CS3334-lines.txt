p1:l10 This course aims to provide students an appreciation to the fundamentals of computer science. Models and applications of data structures
p1:l11 including heaps, search trees, hash tables and disjoint sets are introduced and evaluated. Mathematical tools for analysis of algorithms and
p1:l12 data structures are discussed and applied. Students are given the opportunity to develop and implement applications of the data structures
